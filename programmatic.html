How programmatic cinema works?

Programmatic is an add-on to the traditional offline cinema advertising (DCP) and provides exhibitors and cinema sales houses with new incremental digital revenue. Unless the preshow is already sold out – this is a way of generating additional revenue.

From booking to results

Book cinema advertising like any other digital channel - pick your audience and placement, see your spot on the big screen and follow the results in real time.

  1. 01

    Book the campaign

    Choose spots, creatives, time slots, audience and budget in the programmatic platform, or let our trading desk do it.

  2. 02

    On the big screen

    Your spot plays in the preshow at the cinemas and times you picked, with a voucher for the audience if you want one.

  3. 03

    Follow the results

    See impressions, vouchers, CTR and leads in real-time reporting, and adjust while the campaign runs.
